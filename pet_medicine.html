Pet Medications

Pet Medications

At Playa Compounding Pharmacy, we understand that your pets are part of your family. Just like humans, pets can have unique medical needs that require personalized treatments. We provide compounded pet medications that cater to the specific requirements of your pets, ensuring they get the care they need in a form that’s easy to administer.

Personalized Pet Medications

Animals, like people, may require medication that isn’t commercially available in the right form, strength, or flavor. Our compounding pharmacists work closely with veterinarians to create customized medications that meet the specific needs of your pets, ensuring they receive the best possible care. We compound medications to address a wide range of pet health conditions, including:

  • Pain management for arthritis and injuries
  • Skin conditions (rashes, infections)
  • Behavioral issues (anxiety, aggression)
  • Hormonal imbalances (hyperthyroidism)
  • Seizure control
  • Heart and kidney disease

Take your medication the way you want

We offer pet-friendly medication forms that make it easier to administer treatments, such as:

  • Flavored liquid suspensions (chicken, cod, liver)
  • Transdermal gels for easy absorption
  • Topical creams and ointments for skin conditions
