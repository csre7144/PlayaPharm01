For Healthcare Providers

Partner with Playa Pharmacy

Playa Pharmacy is a PCCA-member compounding pharmacy in Playa del Rey serving prescribers across the Westside. We specialize in non-sterile compounding for hormone therapy, dermatology, pain management, pediatrics, and veterinary medicine.

How to Send Us a Prescription

  • Fax: +1 (310) 823-5700
  • Phone:+1 (310) 823-4500
  • E-Prescribe: Search "Playa Pharmacy" in your e-prescribing system
  • In person: 8131 W. Manchester Ave., Playa del Rey, CA 90293

What to Expect

  • Prescription receipt confirmed to your office within 1 hour
  • Most non-sterile compounds ready within 24 hours
  • We contact your office immediately if we have clinical questions, ingredient concerns, or need clarification — before we fill, not after
  • Patient outcome follow-up: we check in with your office 2–4 weeks after a new compound to report how the patient is responding

Direct Pharmacist Access

Your staff can reach our compounding pharmacist directly by phone at +1 (310) 823-4500. No automated system. No hold queue. Clinical questions about formulations, ingredient alternatives, dosing options, or compound availability are answered directly by the pharmacist who will prepare the medication.

What We Offer Your Practice

  • Prescription templates for our most common formulations — pre-filled with formulation, strength, and directions. Your provider adds patient info and signature.
  • Prior authorization assistance — we handle PA paperwork for medications not covered by insurance and provide pre-completed PA templates your staff only needs to sign.
  • Formulation and dosage consultations — not sure what to compound for a specific clinical scenario? Call us. We consult on formulation and dosage options at no charge.
  • Lunch-and-learn presentations — we offer 20–30 minute educational sessions for your office staff on compounding applications relevant to your specialty.
  • Price range cards — so your staff can give patients approximate cost information at the point of prescribing. No hidden charges.

Our Specialties

  • Hormone Replacement Therapy — BHRT, vaginal health, testosterone replacement
  • Dermatology — allergen-free topicals, combination creams, custom-strength retinoids
  • Pain Management — multi-agent topical analgesics, LDN, neuropathic formulations
  • Pediatrics — flavored suspensions, dye-free formulations, custom dosing
  • Veterinary — flavored treats, topical ear gels, custom-strength preparations
  • Dental — topical anesthetics, dry socket paste, medicated rinses

Quality Standards

We are a PCCA member pharmacy. All compounds are prepared by PCCA-trained pharmacists following USP standards. Ingredients are sourced exclusively from FDA-registered suppliers. We perform three-point verification on every compounded prescription and send samples periodically to certified labs for potency testing.
